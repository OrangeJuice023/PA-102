# Second Brain

## Concepts

### [[Autonomy (CAR vs BARMM)]]

**CAR** = administrative region. **BARMM** = autonomous region.

**Connects:** [[05 Topic 2.4|Topic 2.4]] · [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]] · [[13 Topic 3.7|Topic 3.7]] · [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]]

### [[Bureaucracy (Weber)]]

Bureaucracy = **rational-legal authority** + **division of labor (hierarchy)**.

**Connects:** [[01 Foundational Concepts|Foundational Concepts]] · [[05 Topic 2.4|Topic 2.4]] · [[09 Topic 3.3|Topic 3.3]] · [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]] · [[15 OCT 3 Raw Notes|OCT 3 Raw Notes]]

### [[Constitutional Supremacy]]

The Constitution is the **supreme law** that sets the boundaries of public power. Hierarchy: **Constitution → statutes → administrative rules/orders.**

**Connects:** [[03 Topic 2.1-2.2|Topic 2.1–2.2]] · [[11 Topic 3.5|Topic 3.5]]

### [[De Facto Government]]

**De facto** = "of the fact": it occupies, so it rules (example: Japanese occupation).

**Connects:** [[01 Foundational Concepts|Foundational Concepts]] · [[09 Topic 3.3|Topic 3.3]] · [[10 Topic 3.4|Topic 3.4]]

### [[Decentralization]]

**Decentralization** = transfer of authority from the center to lower levels.

Model: **one nation → one central state**, despite huge regional and ethnolinguistic diversity.

**Connects:** [[07 Topic 3.1|Topic 3.1]] · [[08 Topic 3.2|Topic 3.2]] · [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]]

### [[Diskarte]]

**Morales (2017):** diskarte = **creative problem-solving in response to practical problems and situational constraints**.

**Connects:** [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]]

### [[Due Process]]

**Due process:** violence is no longer private; the **State** uses it through the courts

**Connects:** [[11 Topic 3.5|Topic 3.5]]

### [[Emergency Powers]]

Congress may authorize the President, by law, to exercise powers necessary and proper to carry out a declared national policy during war or another national emergency.

**Connects:** [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]] · [[15 OCT 3 Raw Notes|OCT 3 Raw Notes]]

### [[Federalism]]

Responds to excessive centralization: authority is **constitutionally divided** between national and regional/state governments.

**Connects:** [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]]

### [[Filipino First Policy]]

**Filipino First policy**, started under **Pres. Carlos P. Garcia**: local businesses and products get priority over foreign ones.

**Connects:** [[02 Topic 2|Topic 2]] · [[03 Topic 2.1-2.2|Topic 2.1–2.2]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Hiya]]

**Hiya** = shame; sensitivity to social judgment; concern about embarrassment and relationships.

**Connects:** [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]]

### [[Informal Institutions]]

**Informal institution** = socially established rules, norms, relationships, or practices that shape behavior even when not written into law.

**Connects:** [[03 Topic 2.1-2.2|Topic 2.1–2.2]] · [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]] · [[06 Topic 2.5|Topic 2.5]]

### [[Judicial Review]]

**Expanded judicial review:** courts can review **any branch** for **grave abuse of discretion**.

**Connects:** [[02 Topic 2|Topic 2]] · [[03 Topic 2.1-2.2|Topic 2.1–2.2]] · [[11 Topic 3.5|Topic 3.5]] · [[15 OCT 3 Raw Notes|OCT 3 Raw Notes]]

### [[Malolos Constitution]]

**Malolos Constitution:** free and independent republic, popular sovereignty, representative and responsible government, 3 branches, religious freedom, **separation of Church and State**.

**Connects:** [[08 Topic 3.2|Topic 3.2]] · [[09 Topic 3.3|Topic 3.3]] · [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]]

### [[Martial Law]]

**Martial law** exists to counter paramount force and revolt/insurrection.

**Connects:** [[01 Foundational Concepts|Foundational Concepts]] · [[03 Topic 2.1-2.2|Topic 2.1–2.2]] · [[05 Topic 2.4|Topic 2.4]] · [[09 Topic 3.3|Topic 3.3]] · [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]] · [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]] · [[15 OCT 3 Raw Notes|OCT 3 Raw Notes]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Mestizo Legal System]]

**Main thesis:** **Spanish civil law + American common law + local/customary law = mestizo legal system.**

**Connects:** [[11 Topic 3.5|Topic 3.5]]

### [[Pagdamay]]

The PH has a **pangulo regime** where the **executive is supreme**, rooted in the value of **pagdamay** (fraternity).

**Connects:** [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]]

### [[Pakikisama]]

**Pakikisama** = getting along; maintaining harmonious relationships.

**Connects:** [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]]

### [[Patronage]]

The **patronage / spoils system** (jobs given as political rewards).

**Connects:** [[01 Foundational Concepts|Foundational Concepts]] · [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]] · [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Philippine Constabulary]]

**Philippine Constabulary (1901 to 1991):** a **gendarmerie** (military doing law enforcement) replacing the insular police.

**Connects:** [[09 Topic 3.3|Topic 3.3]]

### [[Police Power]]

**Police power** = the State's power to regulate private conduct, property, and business for **public welfare and self-protection**.

**Connects:** [[11 Topic 3.5|Topic 3.5]]

### [[Separation of Powers]]

Separation of powers **prevents concentrating** legislative, executive, and judicial power in one branch.

**Connects:** [[02 Topic 2|Topic 2]] · [[03 Topic 2.1-2.2|Topic 2.1–2.2]] · [[08 Topic 3.2|Topic 3.2]] · [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]] · [[14 Topic 4.1|Topic 4.1]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

**Sovereignty** = free from external influence; can be **transferred to a successor**.
