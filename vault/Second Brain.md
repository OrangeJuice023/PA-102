### [[Centralization]]

**Centralization** = the concentration of political, administrative, and decision-making authority in the central or national government rather than in lower levels or regions. In the Philippine context, centralized structures were linked to taxation, resource extraction, policy enforcement, and political control.

*Source: PA 102 notes (2–3.7)*

### [[Constitutional Supremacy]]

The Constitution is the **supreme law** that sets the boundaries of public power. Hierarchy: **Constitution → statutes → administrative rules/orders.**

**De facto** = "of the fact": it occupies, so it rules (example: Japanese occupation).

### [[Decentralization]]

**Decentralization** = the transfer of authority, administrative functions, and decision-making from the central government to lower levels of government. It can involve political authority, administrative responsibilities, or economic resources.

*Source: PA 102 notes (2–3.7)*

**Connects:** [[12 Topic 3.6|Topic 3.6]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Diskarte]]

**Diskarte** = creative and adaptive problem-solving in response to practical problems and situational constraints. It involves finding an alternative way to achieve a desired outcome based on the situation. It is not automatically ethical; diskarte can also be used to justify rule-breaking or harmful behavior.

*Source: PA 102 Midterm Reviewer*

**Connects:** [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Due Process]]

**Due Process** = the principle that government must respect a person’s protected liberty and property rights through proper legal procedure, and that the government’s action itself must also be constitutionally valid.

- Procedural due process: “Did the government follow the correct procedure?”
- Substantive due process: “Is the government action itself an excessive or unconstitutional interference?”

*Source: SWX class notes*

**Connects:** [[11 Topic 3.5|Topic 3.5]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Emergency Powers]]

**Emergency Powers** = powers that Congress may authorize the President to exercise by law during war or another national emergency to carry out a declared national policy. The delegation must have restrictions set by Congress and may be withdrawn by Congress.

*Source: Sir's Oct 3 guide*

### [[Federalism]]

**Federalism** = a system in which political authority is constitutionally divided between the national government and regional/state governments, with each having protected powers within its own sphere. It is associated with dual sovereignty and subsidiarity, meaning decisions should be handled at the lowest capable level.

### [[Mestizo Legal System]]

**Mestizo Legal System** = the hybrid Philippine legal system formed by a Spanish Civil Law foundation, an American Common Law and constitutional/public-law overlay, and the continued survival of local/customary law where state penetration was incomplete. “Mestizo” here refers to legal hybridization, not racial mixing.

*Source: PA 102 notes (2–3.7)*

### [[Pagdamay]]

**Pagdamay** = a Filipino value of fraternity, solidarity, and helping or supporting others, especially through shared concern and collective action. In the PA 102 discussion, it helps explain why fraternity became associated with the Filipino pangulo regime.

*Source: Galindez's notes*

### [[Pakikisama]]

**Pakikisama** = the practice of getting along with others and maintaining harmonious social relationships. It can promote cooperation and social cohesion, but becomes problematic when maintaining harmony means avoiding disagreement, covering up wrongdoing, or putting loyalty above accountability.

*Source: PA 102 Midterm Reviewer*

**Connects:** [[04 Topic 2.3|Topic 2.3]] · [[16 Practice Questions|Practice Questions]]

### [[Pangulo Regime]]

**Pangulo Regime** = a political regime characterized by the supremacy of the executive, where the President holds a central position over the state’s many bureaucracies and exercises broad political and administrative influence. Agpalo contrasts it with the parliamentary system’s legislative supremacy and the presidential system’s balance/separation of powers.

*Source: Galindez's notes*

### [[Philippine Constabulary]]

**Philippine Constabulary** = an American-created gendarmerie that combined military and police functions, including law enforcement, suppression of brigandage and insurrection, supervision of local police, and other state-security functions. Its role contributed to the later militarization of Philippine governance discussed in class.

*Source: SWX class notes*

### [[Police Power]]

**Police Power** = the power of the State to regulate private conduct, property, and business for public welfare, safety, order, and self-protection. In the Agabin discussion, it shows how private rights are not absolute when their exercise affects the public or other people.

*Source: SWX class notes*
