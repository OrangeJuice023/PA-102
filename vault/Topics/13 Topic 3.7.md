# Topic 3.7: The unconquered Filipinos

> [!verify] \[NEEDS VERIFICATION\]

> [!plain] In plain words
> the Cordillera and the Bangsamoro were never fully conquered by Spain. The challenge is bringing them into the Filipino state while letting them keep real [[Autonomy (CAR vs BARMM)|autonomy]]. Bangsamoro got an autonomous region (BARMM); the Cordillera is still only an administrative region (CAR).

**Big question:** how can distinct communities with their own identities, territories, and histories be integrated into the Filipino state while keeping **meaningful autonomy**?

## Cordillera

- During Spanish rule, mountainous areas of Luzon stayed outside or only weakly under colonial control. They became the Mountain Province / Cordillera.
- The Cordillera had distinct tribal and community identities and never became one political unit.
- To build a common Filipino identity, communities need **common ground**, but separate tribal identities make political unification hard.
- **Autonomy attempts failed:**

| Year | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **1990** | First attempt; **only Ifugao** approved |
| **1998** | Second attempt; **only Apayao** approved |

- **Why (Galindez):** the tribes **did not trust each other** and didn't want to be governed by each other.
- So there is no Cordillera autonomous region, only the **Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR)**. **Administrative recognition ≠ full constitutional autonomy.**

## Bangsamoro

- Bangsamoro communities had their own political, territorial, and cultural systems **before** being fully incorporated into the Philippine state.
- Colonial and later state policies weakened their control over land and resources.
- **Main chain: land → dispossession → resistance → autonomy.**
  - **Land dispossession:** state policies, settlement, titling, and later commercial and resource development changed who owned and controlled land. Communities lost customary control over territory.
  - **Resistance:** land and political conflicts led to resistance and armed conflict.
  - **Autonomy:** this eventually led to autonomous institutions.
- **ARMM → BARMM:** ARMM was an earlier form of regional autonomy. Later peace processes produced **BARMM** under the **Bangsamoro Organic Law (BOL)**.
- **American legacy (3.3):** the American-drawn map shaped the Bangsamoro territory that Islamic movements fight for.

## Memory

- **Cordillera** = tribes + identity + autonomy.
- **Bangsamoro** = land + dispossession + resistance + autonomy.
- **CAR** = administrative region. **BARMM** = autonomous region.
